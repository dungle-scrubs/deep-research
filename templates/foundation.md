# Foundation search: {{TOPIC}}

First-pass search output on the run topic. Breadth first: map what is
known, who found it, and where the sources disagree. Depth comes later.

## Methodology (non-negotiable for every source reported)

- Report effect sizes, not just significance. Give sample sizes.
- Separate correlation from causation; say which the source supports.
- Name the study population (who, how many, where, when).
- Note funding sources and conflicts of interest where known.
- Record contradictory findings, not just the consensus.
- Mark each finding preliminary vs replicated.
- Give the full source URL for every factual claim. A claim without a URL
  below does not exist for the rest of the pipeline.

## Findings

Organize by sub-question or theme. Every factual statement carries its
source URL inline.

## Open questions and contradictions

What the sources do not settle, and where they point in opposite
directions. The gaps step works from this section.
